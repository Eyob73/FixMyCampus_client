A modern, intuitive campus maintenance and facility issue dashboard for FixMyCampus.

**DESIGN SYSTEM (REQUIRED):**
- Platform: Web, Desktop and Mobile friendly
- Theme: Clean, modern civic-tech light theme
- Background: Very soft cool white (#F8FAFC)
- Surface: White (#FFFFFF) with subtle border (#E2E8F0)
- Primary Accent: Indigo Blue (#3B82F6) with deep cobalt hover (#2563EB)
- Status Colors: Emerald (#10B981) for resolved, Amber (#F59E0B) for in-progress, Crimson (#EF4444) for urgent
- Text Primary: Deep Slate (#0F172A)
- Text Secondary: Slate Muted (#64748B)
- Font: Modern clean sans-serif (Inter)
- Roundness: 8px on buttons/inputs, 12px on cards
- Visual Style: Crisp lines, refined shadows, accessible contrast, informative status badges

**Page Structure:**
1. **Header / Navbar:** Brand logo "FixMyCampus", campus selector dropdown, search bar, "Report New Issue" primary action button, notifications bell, and user avatar.
2. **Metrics Summary:** 4 metric cards:
   - "Active Issues" (count + trend)
   - "In Progress" (count)
   - "Resolved This Week" (count)
   - "Avg Resolution Time" (e.g., 2.4 days)
3. **Quick Filters & Category Bar:** Filter chips for All, Plumbing, Electrical, HVAC/Heating, Safety, Wi-Fi/IT, Grounds.
4. **Recent Incident Feed & Map Toggle:** List/grid of recent campus maintenance tickets showing thumbnail photo, issue title, building location (e.g., "Science Hall Room 304"), urgency tag (Urgent/Medium/Low), status pill (Investigating, Work Scheduled, Resolved), and upvote/me-too counter.
5. **Sidebar / Quick Actions Widget:** "My Reported Issues", campus emergency contact numbers, and quick link to campus maintenance guidelines.
6. **Footer:** University facility services copyright, support email, accessibility statement, and privacy links.
